# Test-day checklist

The PI Behavioral Assessment takes about six minutes, so test day is mostly about choosing a calm moment and reading two instructions properly.

## When the invitation arrives

- [ ] Note the deadline and plan a time before it.
- [ ] Check whether the PI Cognitive Assessment came too. If it did, plan to do the behavioural one first, then take a break before the timed test.
- [ ] Choose your language. PI's walk-through says the first thing you do is confirm the language, then enter your name and email.
- [ ] If you have a disability that affects reading or using a screen, tell the employer. PI lists an English Braille version as a paper-only special order.

## Before you start

- [ ] Find 15 quiet minutes. It is untimed, but you do not want to be interrupted.
- [ ] Use whatever device you like. PI says computers, tablets and phones give equivalent results.
- [ ] Have nothing to look up. There is nothing to look up.

## During the assessment

- [ ] Read the instructions page that explains what to expect.
- [ ] **First list:** tick the adjectives that describe how you are **expected to act by others at work**.
- [ ] **Second list:** tick the adjectives that **really describe you**.
- [ ] Read every word on both lists. Tick every one that fits. There is no maximum.
- [ ] Make sure each list has at least six ticks. PI says fewer makes the result invalid.
- [ ] Go with your first honest reaction. One word does not decide anything.
- [ ] Click Submit after the second list. You will see a confirmation page, and the person who sent the link is notified.

## Afterwards

- [ ] Do not worry about "how you did". PI says there are no right or wrong answers.
- [ ] Prepare a short, true example for each of the four primary factors. Your interviewer may use a PI interview guide built from your results. See [turning your factors into interview answers](study-plans.md#turning-your-factors-into-interview-answers).
- [ ] If you are hired, ask to see your results. Many employers use them for coaching. See [Taking it as an employee](for-employees.md).

## A dry run first

If you have never seen the lists, a five-minute practice run makes the real one feel ordinary: the [free PI Behavioral practice profile on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/pi-behavioral-assessment/practice-profile?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=pi-behavioral-assessment).
