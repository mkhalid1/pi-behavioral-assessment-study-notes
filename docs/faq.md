# FAQ

Answers to the questions people most often search for about the PI Behavioral Assessment. The questions come from Google's "People also ask" results; the answers come from PI's own pages, listed in [Sources](sources.md).

## What is the PI Behavioral Assessment?

PI describes it as a self-report measure of adult work-related personality. It is untimed, takes about six minutes on average, and asks you to tick adjectives on two lists. It measures four primary drives (Dominance, Extraversion, Patience and Formality) and a secondary decision-making factor, Objectivity. See [the assessment explained](test-overview.md).

## What is a Predictive Index (PI) assessment?

"PI assessment" can mean either of The Predictive Index's two candidate assessments: this behavioural one, or the timed 50-question [PI Cognitive Assessment](https://github.com/mkhalid1/pi-cognitive-assessment-study-notes). Employers often send both.

## What kind of questions are on the PI Behavioral Assessment?

Only two. Each is followed by a list of adjectives. The first asks you to tick the words that describe how you are expected to act by others at work. The second asks you to tick the words that really describe you. There is no maximum. See [The two lists](sections/the-two-lists.md).

## What is an example of a PI behavioral assessment question?

The real instructions are, in short: "Select the adjectives that describe the way you are expected to act by others at work," and "Select the adjectives that you feel really describe you." Then you tick from a list. PI's walk-through gives the example of someone doing a lot of mathematical work feeling that others expect them to be patient, rational or analytical. The worked examples in this repo show how to apply the two instructions.

## How does the Predictive Index Behavioral Assessment work?

Each adjective relates to one of PI's factors, though PI does not publish which. Your choices are scored within you, so the result shows the relative strength of your own drives rather than ranking you against others. The result is a pattern, plus one of 17 Reference Profiles. See [How results are scored and read](scoring.md).

## How is the Predictive Index Behavioral Assessment scored?

Within the person. PI looks at which of your factors is highest, which is lowest, and how far apart they are, drawn around a midpoint. Factors near the midpoint are more adaptable; factors far from it are expressed more strongly. Employers compare your pattern with a behavioural target for the job, as a match score.

## Can you fail a PI behavioral assessment?

No. PI says it is not pass or fail and has no right or wrong answers. The only ways to get an unusable result are choosing fewer than six words, or more than 80, in a list. What an employer does with the result is a separate question: they compare it with a target, and PI tells them to use it as one data point among several.

## What is a good PI score?

There is no good or bad score on the behavioural assessment. PI says high and low on each factor are neither good nor bad. A pattern can be closer to or further from a particular job's target, which is a matter of fit, not quality. For the cognitive test, which does have scores, see the [PI Cognitive study notes](https://github.com/mkhalid1/pi-cognitive-assessment-study-notes).

## How should I answer the Predictive Index?

Honestly, and quickly. Read each instruction, tick every word that fits, and go with your first reaction. Put job expectations on the first list and your nature on the second. PI's own research summary says faking rarely works, because you cannot see which word relates to which factor, and because you would need the right combination of words, not just flattering ones.

## How do I do well on a PI assessment?

On the behavioural one, "doing well" means giving an accurate picture of yourself. Understand the two lists, answer honestly, and prepare a true example for each factor to use in the interview. On the cognitive one, practice with the formats and pacing genuinely helps.

## What is the rarest Predictive Index profile?

PI does not publish how common each Reference Profile is on the pages we checked. It also says profiles are not used in fit calculations or hiring decisions, so rarity does not affect your chances. See [The 17 Reference Profiles](reference-profiles.md).

## What Predictive Index profile is best for a CEO?

PI does not name one. It warns against a "one size fits all" approach, saying the same role can need different behaviours in different companies and that several profiles can succeed in the same role.

## What is my Predictive Index behavioral type?

PI calls these Reference Profiles. There are 17, in four groups: Analytical, Social, Stabilizing and Persistent. You are placed in the one whose prototype pattern is closest to yours. See [The 17 Reference Profiles](reference-profiles.md).

## How is the PI Behavioral Assessment used in hiring?

Employers set a behavioural target for the job, compare candidates with it as a match score, and use an interview guide generated from each candidate's results. PI says the BA is valid for hiring, but should be used alongside interviews and other information.

## What else is the PI Behavioral Assessment used for?

PI says the same results are used after hiring for coaching, development, team building and change management. See [Taking it as an employee](for-employees.md).

## How long does it take?

It is untimed. PI says it takes about six minutes on average.

## Can I take it on my phone?

Yes. PI compared thousands of results from computers, tablets and phones and found the device makes no difference.

## Can I retake it?

PI recommends no more than once a year, and only when there is a reason. Its research says results stay stable for years.

## How good is the Predictive Index? Is it scientifically valid?

PI says the BA has been in commercial use since 1955, was last revised in 2017, and has been certified under the European Federation of Psychologists' Associations (EFPA) review model, first in 2018 and again in 2021. PI's reliability and validity page says it has run over 400 client validation studies. These are PI's own claims about its assessment.

## In which languages is it available?

PI's history page says the BA is available in 68 languages, including English Braille (paper only). Ask the employer which one they can send.

## Where can I practise?

Read the worked examples in this repo, starting with [The two lists](sections/the-two-lists.md), and do a dry run with the [free PI Behavioral practice profile on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/pi-behavioral-assessment/practice-profile?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=pi-behavioral-assessment).
