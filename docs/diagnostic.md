# 10-minute self-check

Twelve original questions, two on each part of the assessment. There is nothing to "pass" on the real PI Behavioral Assessment, so this is not a mock test. It checks how well you understand the format and what your results will mean, which is what lets you answer the real thing clearly and talk about your results with confidence.

These items are original, written for these notes. They are not taken from PI's materials.

Give yourself about 7 minutes, then check your answers and use the table at the bottom.

If you would rather see the format first, try the [free PI Behavioral practice profile on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/pi-behavioral-assessment/practice-profile?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=pi-behavioral-assessment), then come back.

## Part A: The two lists

**D1.** Which list builds your Self-Concept pattern?
A) The first list: how you are expected to act by others at work
B) The second list: what really describes you
C) Both lists combined
D) Neither: it comes from your CV

**D2.** What is the fewest words PI says you must choose in each list for a valid result?
A) 1  B) 3  C) 6  D) 20

## Part B: Dominance

**D3.** Whose needs are "encouragement, reassurance and harmony", according to PI?
A) People with high Dominance
B) People with low Dominance
C) People with high Formality
D) People with low Patience

**D4.** Is a high Dominance result better than a low one?
A) Yes, always
B) Only for managers
C) No. PI says high and low are neither good nor bad.
D) Only if Extraversion is also high

## Part C: Extraversion

**D5.** Which needs go with low Extraversion, according to PI?
A) Independence and challenge
B) Privacy, time to think and room for reflection
C) Clear expectations
D) Variety and change

**D6.** B sits clearly higher than D. How does PI describe this?
A) Formal
B) Informal
C) Cautious with risk
D) Responsive

## Part D: Patience

**D7.** Which needs go with low Patience, according to PI?
A) Long-term affiliation and a steady pace
B) Privacy and reflection
C) Variety, change and freedom from repetition
D) Clear rules

**D8.** C sits clearly higher than B. How does PI describe this?
A) Quick to connect
B) Proactive
C) Takes time to connect
D) Informal

## Part E: Formality

**D9.** Which needs go with high Formality, according to PI?
A) Flexibility and freedom from rules
B) Encouragement and harmony
C) Interaction and connection
D) Specific job knowledge, freedom from risk or error, and clear expectations

**D10.** C sits clearly higher than D. How does PI say this person sees rules?
A) As guidelines: casual with rules
B) As essential: careful with rules
C) As a risk
D) It cannot be read

## Part F: Objectivity

**D11.** What is Factor E?
A) A fifth primary drive, measured like the others
B) A secondary factor about how someone makes decisions
C) An error score
D) The number of words chosen

**D12.** Can two people with almost the same A, B, C and D have different decision-making styles?
A) No
B) Yes, because Factor E is separate and can differ
C) Only if they used different devices
D) Only after a retake

<details>
<summary>Answers and explanations, D1 to D12</summary>

**D1: A.** The first list asks how you are expected to act by others at work. PI calls the result your Self-Concept.

**D2: C.** PI says the BA only works with at least six words in each list. Fewer, and the results are not valid.

**D3: B.** PI says people with low Dominance need encouragement, reassurance and harmony.

**D4: C.** PI says there are no right or wrong answers, and that low and high are neither good nor bad.

**D5: B.** PI says people with low Extraversion need privacy, time to think and room for reflection.

**D6: B.** PI says B higher than D means informal: friendly, expressive and uninhibited.

**D7: C.** PI says people with low Patience need variety, change and freedom from repetition.

**D8: C.** PI says C higher than B means someone who takes time to connect, building relationships over time.

**D9: D.** PI says people with high Formality need specific job knowledge, freedom from risk or error, and clear expectations.

**D10: A.** PI says C higher than D means casual with rules, seeing rules more as guidelines.

**D11: B.** PI calls Factor E a secondary, modifying factor that describes whether someone relies more on subjective or objective information when deciding.

**D12: B.** Because E is separate from A to D, PI says two people can share a pattern and still decide differently.

</details>
