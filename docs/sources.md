## What is our own guidance

These parts are our advice, not PI's: the worked examples and the people in them, the methods and traps lists, the study plans, the self-check thresholds, the suggestion to do the behavioural assessment before the cognitive one, and the interview-story method. PI does not publish how many adjectives the lists contain or which factor each word relates to, and these notes do not guess.
