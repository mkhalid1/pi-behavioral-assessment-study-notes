# Study plans

You cannot study your way to a "better" PI Behavioral result, and PI says you should not try. What preparation does is make the format familiar, so you answer clearly, and help you understand your results, so you can talk about them in the interview. Both plans start with the [15-minute self-check](diagnostic.md).

## 7-day plan

About 15 to 20 minutes a day. Useful if you want to understand PI properly, for example because your employer uses it for coaching, or you have a series of interviews with PI users.

| Day | Focus | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The format | Read [the assessment explained](test-overview.md) and take the [self-check](diagnostic.md). Read [The two lists](sections/the-two-lists.md) and do its worked examples. |
| 2 | Dominance and Extraversion | Read the [Dominance](sections/dominance.md) and [Extraversion](sections/extraversion.md) guides. Do both sets of worked examples. |
| 3 | Patience and Formality | Read the [Patience](sections/patience.md) and [Formality](sections/formality.md) guides. Note where the everyday meaning of each word differs from PI's. |
| 4 | Objectivity and results | Read the [Objectivity guide](sections/objectivity.md) and [How results are scored and read](scoring.md). |
| 5 | A dry run | Build a practice pattern with the [free PI Behavioral practice profile on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/pi-behavioral-assessment/practice-profile?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=pi-behavioral-assessment). Answer as you would on the day: honestly, first reaction, every word that fits. |
| 6 | Reflect | Read [The 17 Reference Profiles](reference-profiles.md). For each of the four primary factors, write one real example from your work that shows how you like to operate. These are your interview stories. |
| 7 | Rest | Read the [test-day checklist](test-day-checklist.md). Take the real assessment when you are calm and have 15 free minutes. |

### Turning your factors into interview answers

PI's software gives employers an interview guide based on your results, so expect questions that probe your pattern. For each factor, prepare one short, true story:

1. **The need.** "I do my best work when I have time to think before I respond."
2. **A real example.** A specific time it showed up at work.
3. **How you adapt.** What you do when the job asks for the opposite, for example preparing notes before a meeting so you can contribute early.

This is honest, specific and far more convincing than trying to match a profile.

## 2-day plan

For when the link has arrived.

### Day 1: understand the format

| Block | Time | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 10 min | Take the [self-check](diagnostic.md). |
| 2 | 10 min | Read [The two lists](sections/the-two-lists.md) and do its worked examples. |
| 3 | 10 min | Skim "What it measures" in each of the five factor guides. |
| 4 | 10 min | Do a dry run with the [PrepClubs practice profile](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/pi-behavioral-assessment/practice-profile?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=pi-behavioral-assessment). |

### Day 2: take it

| Block | Time | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5 min | Read the [test-day checklist](test-day-checklist.md). |
| 2 | 15 min | Take the real assessment in a quiet moment. Read each instruction, tick every word that really fits, submit. |

If the employer also sent the PI Cognitive Assessment, do this one first, take a break, then do the timed cognitive test. See the [PI Cognitive Assessment study notes](https://github.com/mkhalid1/pi-cognitive-assessment-study-notes).

## What not to do

- **Do not hunt for "the right words".** PI does not publish which adjective relates to which factor, removes words that are obviously desirable, and scores within the person, so you would need the right combination as well as the right words. It almost never works.
- **Do not copy a profile from the internet.** PI says Reference Profiles are not used for hiring decisions or fit calculations.
- **Do not take it repeatedly to "improve" it.** PI recommends no more than once a year.
- **Do not overthink.** First honest reactions give the clearest pattern.
