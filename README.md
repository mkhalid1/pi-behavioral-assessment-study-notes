# PI Behavioral Assessment Study Notes

A free, complete guide to the Predictive Index (PI) Behavioral Assessment: how the two adjective lists work, what each of the five factors means at both ends, how your pattern, Reference Profile and match score are worked out, and how to talk about your results in an interview. It includes 42 original worked examples with full explanations, a self-check, and plans for a week or a weekend. It is written for job candidates who have just received a PI link, with a page for employees asked to take it for coaching. This is not a guide to "gaming" the assessment. PI says that does not work, and the notes explain why. Facts come only from The Predictive Index's own pages, each one [cited](docs/sources.md).

Want to see the format first? Try the [free PI Behavioral practice profile on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/pi-behavioral-assessment/practice-profile?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=pi-behavioral-assessment), or the [10-minute self-check](docs/diagnostic.md) in this repo.

## Start here

1. **Check what you already know.** Take the [10-minute self-check](docs/diagnostic.md). It has two questions on each part of the assessment.
2. **Learn the two lists.** Read [The two lists](docs/sections/the-two-lists.md). It is the one page that directly affects how accurate your result will be.
3. **Follow a plan.** Use the [7-day plan](docs/study-plans.md#7-day-plan) to understand your results in depth, or the [2-day plan](docs/study-plans.md#2-day-plan) if the link has already arrived.

## Quick facts

| | |
|---|---|
| Publisher | The Predictive Index (PI) |
| Format | Two questions, each with a list of adjectives. Tick every word that applies. |
| Time | Untimed. About six minutes on average. |
| Pass mark | None. PI says it is not pass or fail and has no right or wrong answers. |
| Primary factors | Dominance (A), Extraversion (B), Patience (C), Formality (D) |
| Secondary factor | Objectivity (E): how you make decisions |
| Scoring | Within the person: how your own drives compare with each other |
| Validity rule | At least 6 words in each list. Over 80 makes results uninterpretable. |
| Result | A pattern, one of 17 Reference Profiles, and a match score against the job's target |
| Device | Computer, tablet or phone. PI says results are equivalent. |
| Retakes | PI recommends no more than once a year |

Sources: [PI Behavioral Assessment](https://www.predictiveindex.com/assessments/behavioral-assessment/) and [Introduction to the PI Behavioral Assessment](https://www.predictiveindex.com/learn/support/introduction-to-the-pi-behavioral-assessment/), checked 24 November 2026.

| Guide | What it covers | Guide |
|---|---|---|

| Dominance (A) | The drive to exert influence on people or events | [Dominance](docs/sections/dominance.md) |
| Extraversion (B) | The drive for social interaction at work | [Extraversion](docs/sections/extraversion.md) |
| Patience (C) | The drive for consistency and stability | [Patience](docs/sections/patience.md) |
| Formality (D) | The drive to conform to rules and structure | [Formality](docs/sections/formality.md) |

Every guide has the same parts: what it measures, how the questions look, a method for answering and for reading your result, common misreadings, timing, 5 original worked examples with explanations, and a score table.

## How to use this repo

- **Read on GitHub.** Every page has links to the previous page, the next page and back to these contents, so you can read it start to finish like a book.
- **Treat the worked examples as a check on understanding.** Each has one best answer based on PI's published guidance. Answers are hidden under "Answers and explanations".
- **Read every explanation.** Most say why the nearest wrong option is wrong, which is usually a common misreading of PI's terms.
- **Then answer the real thing honestly.** Knowing the factors helps you understand and discuss your results. It does not tell you what to tick, and these notes never suggest words to pick.
- **Do a dry run.** The adjective lists feel unusual the first time. A practice run, such as the [free one on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/pi-behavioral-assessment/practice-profile?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=pi-behavioral-assessment), removes the surprise.
- **Clone or fork it** if you want your own copy. The structure also works as a GitBook space: see [SUMMARY.md](SUMMARY.md).

## About the worked examples

All 42 worked examples (30 in the guides and 12 in the self-check) are original, written for these notes. None are taken from PI's materials or from any prep site. They test how the assessment works and how PI describes each factor. They do not map adjectives to factors: PI does not publish that mapping, and these notes do not guess it.

## Also taking the cognitive test?

Many employers send the timed PI Cognitive Assessment with this one. It is a completely different test, with right answers and a 12-minute limit. See the companion [PI Cognitive Assessment Study Notes](https://github.com/mkhalid1/pi-cognitive-assessment-study-notes).
